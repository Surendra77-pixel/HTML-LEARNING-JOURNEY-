In this example, we have used six different heading levels, from <h1> to <h6>. The <h1> tag represents the most important heading, while the <h6> tag represents the least important heading. Using headings helps to structure your content and improve readability for users and search engines.

note - The browser automatically add some white space(A margin) before and after a heading.
